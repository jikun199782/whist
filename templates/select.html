Blackjack

Card game where players aim to get a hand value of 21 or less, competing against the dealer without busting. Face cards count as 10.

Blackjack_eu

European version of the standard Blackjack, where the dealer receives only one card initially and does not check for blackjack until the player has acted.

Whist (AI Mode)

Classic four-player trick-taking card game. You (South) play against three AI opponents in fixed partnerships. First team to 7 tricks wins.

Whist (Multiplayer)

Play Whist with friends! Create a game, share the link, and up to 3 friends can join to play as North, East, and West. Empty seats filled by AI.
